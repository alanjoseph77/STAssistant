# Privacy Policy – DeskMate

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

DeskMate is an Android app that connects to your Smart Table Assistant desk companion over Bluetooth Low Energy.

## What the app accesses

| Access | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Bluetooth (nearby devices) | To find and connect to your watch. |
| Notifications (notification access) | To show your phone's notifications on the watch. |
| Phone state, call log, contacts | To show who is calling (name or number) on the watch while the phone rings. |
| Foreground service and notification | To keep the Bluetooth connection alive while the screen is off. |
| Battery optimization exemption | So Android does not stop the connection in the background. |

## What happens to your data

- Calls and notifications are **only sent to your own watch over Bluetooth**, as short text, at the moment they arrive.
- The app **does not store, log, sell or share** your notifications, calls or contacts.
- The app **has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no servers**. It does not send any data to the internet.
- Everything stays on your phone and your watch. Removing the app removes all of it.

## Your choices

You can turn call and notification forwarding off inside the app, and revoke any permission at any time in
Android Settings.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
